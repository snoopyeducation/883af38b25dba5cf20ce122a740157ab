2026 Wichita Public Schools bond

The 2026 Bond is an opportunity for our community to come together and build for the future. WPS knows that Wichita values a strong community within its schools. 

Read More about 2026 Wichita Public Schools bond
Nominate a WPS teacher for the Distinguished Classroom Teacher Award

Nominations are open for the Distinguished Classroom Teacher Award. This prestigious recognition honors certified staff members in Wichita Public Schools who demonstrate exceptional skill in instruction, leadership, and service.
 

Read More about Nominate a WPS teacher for the Distinguished Classroom Teacher Award
Bond proposal would rebuild Coleman Middle School, expanded student supports

If the bond questions pass, a new Coleman Middle School would be rebuilt with modern learning spaces. The existing building would be renovated to bring Chester Lewis Alternative High School, Education Imagine Academy and the Gateway Alternative Program together, reducing facility costs and providing more consistent student supports.

Read More about Bond proposal would rebuild Coleman Middle School, expanded student supports
My WPS Story - Chris Jackson

Wichita content creator Chris Jackson, a former WPS student, reflects on how his experiences as a student shaped his creative journey. In his My WPS Story, he encourages today’s students to explore new opportunities and trust that they do not need to have everything figured out yet.

Read More about My WPS Story - Chris Jackson
Behind Every School Meal: The Team Fueling Wichita Public Schools

Each school day, WPS Nutrition Services serves meals to about 27,000 students, a number greater than the populations of Derby, Great Bend and Garden City. Assistant Director Adrea Katzenmeier shares how the team works together to order, prepare and deliver the meals students depend on to learn and thrive.

Read More about Behind Every School Meal: The Team Fueling Wichita Public Schools
Bond proposal could transform Cessna Elementary into a K-8

Community feedback helped shape a proposal to add a middle school wing at Cessna Elementary, allowing students to continue learning in a familiar environment with trusted staff through eighth grade. 

Read More about Bond proposal could transform Cessna Elementary into a K-8
WPS bond: Chisholm Trail Elementary could become a modern K-8 school

If the Wichita Public Schools bond passes, Chisholm Trail Elementary would be rebuilt as a modern K-8 school, replacing an aging building that no longer efficiently serves today’s needs.

Read More about WPS bond: Chisholm Trail Elementary could become a modern K-8 school
Middle school students bring technology to life

At Marshall, Brooks and Pleasant Valley middle schools, students are using technology to build, experiment and solve problems. From robotics and 3D printing to everyday digital tools, the focus is on what students create and learn.

Read More about Middle school students bring technology to life
Wichita Public Schools 26-27 enrollment is 43,643 students

The Wichita Public Schools’ official student enrollment for the 26-27 school year is 43,643 students.  

Read More about Wichita Public Schools 26-27 enrollment is 43,643 students
WPS bond to address traffic issues at Christa McAuliffe Academy and Isely K-8

At Christa McAuliffe Academy and Isely K-8 Traditional Magnet, traffic during drop-off and pick-up backs up into nearby neighborhoods and puts vehicles and students in the same crowded space. The 2026 bond proposal would move traffic flow onto WPS property, making it smoother for schools and better for neighbors.

Read More about WPS bond to address traffic issues at Christa McAuliffe Academy and Isely K-8
